Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook (SAMPLE)
This is a fictional document created to demonstrate the "chat with your documents" mode. Northwind Labs is not a real company.

1. Working hours
Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone is expected to be available, are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Employees may start between 07:30 and 10:00.

2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing probation. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head. Remote employees must be reachable on the company chat during core hours.

3. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus 1 extra day for every 3 completed years of service, up to a maximum of 23 days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used before 31 March. Leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

4. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive working days.

5. Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 26 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of paid leave, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

6. Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 90 days. During probation, either party may end the employment with 5 working days' notice.

7. Resignation and notice period
After probation, employees must give 30 days' written notice before resigning. Employees in management roles must give 60 days' notice.

8. Business expenses
Approved business travel expenses are reimbursed within 15 working days. Meal expenses during business travel are covered up to 40 USD per day. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the expense.

9. Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop, which must be returned on the last working day. Personal devices may access company email only after enrolling in the device management system.

10. Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,000 USD for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
